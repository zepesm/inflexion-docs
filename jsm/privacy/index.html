This Privacy Policy describes how Inflexion Krzysztof Augustyn (“we”, “us”) processes information in connection with the Atlassian Marketplace cloud app commonly referred to as ReplyMerge for Jira Service Management / jsm-email-dedupe (the “App”), a Forge application for Jira Service Management.

1. Role of the parties

  • The App is installed by an Atlassian customer (the “Customer”) on their Atlassian Cloud site.
  • The Customer is typically the controller of End-User Data in their Jira / JSM instance.
  • We provide the App as a Marketplace Partner. Processing by the App occurs primarily inside Atlassian’s Forge platform under the Customer’s installation.

See also Atlassian’s developer and Marketplace terms: Atlassian Developer Terms.

2. What the App processes

The App may read and write the following categories of data within the Customer’s Atlassian Cloud site, as needed to detect and merge email-reply duplicates:

CategoryExamplesWhere it lives
Issue / request fieldsSummary, description, status, resolution, created time, project keyJira / JSM
People identifiersReporter and participant Atlassian account IDs, display names; email addresses only if exposed by Jira APIs the App is allowed to callJira / JSM
Comments & attachmentsComment bodies, visibility (public/internal), attachment metadata and content (re-uploaded on merge; oversized skipped)Jira / JSM
Channel / propertiesrequest.channel.type, App-owned issue properties (detection + merge audit)Jira issue properties
Workflow metadataStatuses and transition graph (read-only close-path pre-check)Jira workflow APIs

The App does not intend to receive raw email Message-ID / In-Reply-To / References headers (those are not exposed to Forge apps for this use case).

3. Storage outside Atlassian products

The App does not store End-User Data on vendor-operated servers. Persistence is via:

  • Jira issue entity properties (detection and merge audit), and
  • Forge app KVS (storage:app) for the per-site auto-merge toggle and change audit (admin account id + timestamp) — Atlassian-hosted.

Answer for Marketplace Privacy & Security tab:
“Does your app store End-User Data outside of Atlassian products and services?” → No.
See also: Runs on Atlassian.

4. Egress / subprocessors

The App declares no remotes and no permissions.external fetch/egress. Runtime compute is Forge.

Subprocessors today: Atlassian (Forge / Jira Cloud). No other subprocessors planned for MVP.

5. Purposes

  • Detect likely duplicate JSM requests created from email replies
  • Diagnose probable causes
  • Optionally merge content into the original request and close the duplicate
  • Maintain an audit trail for admins/agents

6. Legal bases (EU/EEA — high level)

Where GDPR applies, processing is based on the Customer’s instructions and legitimate interests / contract with the Customer as applicable. Exact Article 6 mapping and any need for a separate DPA with customers should be confirmed with counsel.

7. Retention

  • Issue properties and comments remain in the Customer’s Jira site under the Customer’s retention practices.
  • On uninstall, Forge app data is subject to Atlassian’s uninstall/data handling for Forge apps.

8. International transfers

Data processed by Forge / Jira Cloud is hosted by Atlassian according to the Customer’s site and data residency configuration. We do not operate a separate transfer of End-User Data to Inflexion Krzysztof Augustyn systems in the MVP architecture.

9. Security

We follow Atlassian’s Security requirements for cloud apps applicable to Forge apps (least-privilege scopes, no storage of user passwords/API tokens, dependency hygiene, etc.). See also our Security & data handling page.
